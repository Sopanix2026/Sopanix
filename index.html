Content helper

Change text and team details, then download an updated site.json.

Important: This is not a secure CMS and cannot publish by itself. Download the JSON, replace content/site.json in GitHub and commit the change. Direct image upload and secure login require a separately configured CMS service.

Founder & team

Image/logo guide

Upload your logo or portraits into assets/uploads/ in GitHub. Use the exact path in the Image path field, e.g. /assets/uploads/founder.jpg. Replace assets/uploads/logo.svg to use your own logo.

Publish steps

  1. Download site.json.
  2. Open Sopanix/content/site.json in GitHub and choose Edit.
  3. Replace its contents and commit to main.
  4. Wait for Netlify deployment.
